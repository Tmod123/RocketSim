# Rocket Flight Simulator

**Moe and Gene Johnson High School — Aerospace Class**

*Computational Aerospace Engineering Project*

---

## Project Overview

The Rocket Flight Simulator is a computational aerospace engineering project developed by the Moe and Gene Johnson High School Aerospace Class. The simulator models rocket flight using numerical methods and physical principles to investigate flight dynamics, aerodynamic forces, and the effects of environmental conditions on rocket performance.

The project integrates physics, mathematics, and programming to provide a computational platform for analyzing rocket flight and exploring the relationships between vehicle properties, atmospheric conditions, and flight outcomes.

## Project Objectives

- Develop a computational model of rocket flight.
- Model the effects of thrust, gravity, aerodynamic drag, and atmospheric conditions.
- Visualize flight performance through trajectory plots and time-dependent simulation data.
- Investigate the effects of input uncertainty on predicted flight outcomes using Monte Carlo analysis.

## Technical Overview

The simulator uses mathematical models and numerical integration to calculate rocket motion over the course of a simulated flight.

Its modeling framework incorporates relevant physical parameters, including rocket mass, motor thrust, aerodynamic characteristics, and environmental conditions.

Monte Carlo analysis evaluates how variations in selected input parameters influence simulated performance and the distribution of predicted outcomes.

## Results and Analysis

The simulator produces numerical outputs and visualizations for analyzing rocket flight behavior. These outputs can be used to examine changes in altitude, velocity, acceleration, and trajectory throughout a simulated flight.

Statistical analysis of repeated simulations provides additional insight into the sensitivity of predicted outcomes to variations in model inputs.
